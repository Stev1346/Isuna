# Financial Disclaimer

> **Visibility:** Public  
> **Status:** Founding MVP documentation; capabilities may not yet be available  
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-16


## Organizational tool only

Isuna is designed to help users organize, enter, import, categorize, calculate, review and report information. It is a software tool and does not replace a qualified professional.

## No professional advice

Information, calculations, dashboards, scores, projections, scenarios, educational explanations and reports produced by Isuna are **not** financial, investment, tax, legal, accounting, credit, insurance, retirement, estate-planning or mortgage advice.

Isuna does not act as a financial planner, investment adviser, portfolio manager, accountant, tax preparer, lawyer, mortgage broker, lender, bank, credit bureau, insurance adviser or fiduciary.

## No recommendation or suitability assessment

Isuna does not know all facts that may affect a household’s decisions. A category, debt order, savings target, withdrawal-rate scenario, financial-independence estimate, mortgage calculation or health score does not mean a strategy is suitable, optimal or safe for a particular person.

Users should consult appropriately qualified professionals for advice about their circumstances.

## User-entered and third-party information

Results depend on the completeness, accuracy, timing and classification of information entered, imported or received from third parties. Users remain responsible for checking source statements and correcting errors. Reconciliation and freshness indicators can help, but they do not guarantee that records are complete.

## Estimates and assumptions

Debt payoff dates, interest, mortgage amortization, property values, savings suggestions, investment growth, financial-independence targets and withdrawal scenarios are estimates based on stated assumptions. Actual rates, fees, taxes, market returns, inflation, payment timing, lender methods, product terms and personal circumstances may differ materially.

Past performance and a projected result do not guarantee a future result. No investment return or debt-payoff outcome is guaranteed.

## Financial health score

Any Isuna financial health score is intended to be a transparent organizational summary based on selected product data. It is not a credit score, risk rating, eligibility decision, professional assessment or prediction of financial success.

## Canadian-first, not universal

Canadian terminology or defaults do not mean the product accounts for every federal, provincial, territorial, tax, legal or financial rule. Laws and programs change, and users are responsible for obtaining current professional guidance.

## No transactions or custody

The founding product does not hold funds, execute trades, pay bills, transfer money or make lending decisions. Links or integrations with external providers remain subject to those providers’ terms and records.

## Decisions remain the user’s responsibility

Users are solely responsible for decisions and actions taken using information from Isuna. Where the application shows a calculation or scenario, review its definition, inputs, assumptions, date and limitations before relying on it.

## Seek urgent or specialized help

Users facing insolvency, fraud, identity theft, legal deadlines, tax issues, foreclosure, investment losses or other serious circumstances should seek appropriate professional or emergency assistance rather than relying solely on Isuna.
