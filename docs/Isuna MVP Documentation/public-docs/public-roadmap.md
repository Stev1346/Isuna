# Public Roadmap

> **Visibility:** Public  
> **Status:** Founding MVP documentation; capabilities may not yet be available  
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-16


## How to read this roadmap

This roadmap describes direction, not promised release dates. Priorities may change as the founder learns from users, security reviews, technical evidence and regulatory/privacy guidance. A phase begins only when its foundations are reliable.

## Phase 1 — Founding MVP

Build the complete manual household operating loop:

- secure accounts and household collaboration;
- guided financial setup;
- accounts, transactions and CSV import;
- budgets and reconciliation;
- debt, savings and manual investment tracking;
- executive dashboard;
- Monthly Money Meeting;
- immutable month close, reports and quarterly review;
- export, deletion, monitoring and launch safeguards.

## Phase 2 — Automation and convenience

Reduce repetitive work through improved rules, recurring-item support, saved import mappings, reminders and faster review. Automation must remain explainable and reversible.

## Phase 3 — Connected financial accounts

Evaluate Canadian account-data providers and open-banking options. Connection health, consent, duplicates, reconciliation and provider outages must be designed carefully before this becomes a public feature.

## Phase 4 — Advanced planning

Explore deeper debt, mortgage, goal and financial-independence scenarios with transparent assumptions and independent calculation review. Isuna will remain an organizational tool rather than an advice service.

## Phase 5 — Mobile experience

Improve mobile and progressive-web workflows first. A native app would be considered only if user needs cannot be met responsibly by the web experience.

## Phase 6 — Professional collaboration

Explore household-authorized, time-limited collaboration with qualified professionals. This would require a new permission model, privacy review and market validation.

## Phase 7 — International support

Expand only to specific countries with local terminology, currency/accounting rules, privacy/legal review, support capacity and provider coverage.

## Phase 8 — Privacy-conscious assistance

Explore carefully scoped assistance such as navigation, categorization suggestions or meeting-summary drafting. Users must remain in control, important actions require confirmation, and the product must not present generated content as professional financial advice.

## Not on the roadmap

Isuna is not planning to become a bank, payment-transfer service, tax filer, lender, brokerage, credit bureau or advertising-data business.
