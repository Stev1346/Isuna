# Isuna Public Documentation

> **Visibility:** Public  
> **Status:** Founding MVP documentation; capabilities may not yet be available  
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-16


## What Isuna is

Isuna is a Canadian-first **Family Financial Operating System** being designed to help individuals, couples and households organize their complete financial picture, review it regularly, make decisions and preserve a dependable history.

**Product promise:** See your whole financial picture, know what needs attention, and decide what comes next—together.

## Who it is for

Isuna is intended for households that want more structure than separate bank apps or a fragile spreadsheet. It may be useful for people organizing accounts, coordinating finances with a partner, paying down debt, building savings, tracking investments manually or working toward long-term goals.

## The problem it addresses

Financial information is often spread across institutions, spreadsheets and conversations. Transaction lists can show what happened without explaining whether the month is complete, what changed, what needs attention or what the household decided to do next.

## Core benefits under development

- A connected view of accounts, debts, savings, investments and net worth.
- Monthly budgets with actual spending and variance.
- Account reconciliation and data-freshness indicators.
- A guided Monthly Money Meeting.
- Decisions and action items for the household.
- Immutable month-end snapshots and PDF reports.
- Quarterly reviews built from closed monthly records.
- Shared household access with clear roles.
- Data export and deletion controls.

## Development status

Isuna is in founding MVP development. This documentation describes intended direction and does not mean every feature is available. The public roadmap avoids release-date promises. Security, privacy, accessibility and data integrity are being treated as launch requirements rather than later additions.

## Name and origin

Isuna is inspired by **Ìṣúná**, the Yoruba word for “budget,” reflecting the founder’s Yoruba and Nigerian heritage. The product’s visual identity is intended to be modern, calm and respectful without relying on cultural stereotypes.

Isuna is an organizational, planning and reporting tool. It does not provide professional financial, investment, tax, legal, accounting, credit or mortgage advice, and it does not guarantee any financial result. See [`financial-disclaimer.md`](financial-disclaimer.md).
